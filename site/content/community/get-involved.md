Buildish is looking for feedback on project structure, documentation,
workflows, and tooling while the components move toward their first releases.

## Ways to contribute

- Review the pre-release components and share what works or what feels rough.
- Email the [development contact](mailto:dev@buildish.org) to discuss ideas and priorities.
- Browse the [source repository](https://github.com/buildish-tooling/buildish) for issues, pull requests, and project structure.
- Improve docs, examples, naming, onboarding notes, and contributor guidance.

## Early focus areas

Early contributors can help validate the aggregate website, shape CI
integration patterns, improve build automation ergonomics, and make component
documentation easier to use.
